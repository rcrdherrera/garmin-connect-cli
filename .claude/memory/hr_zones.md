## Methodology

**Framework:** 5-zone dual-threshold model (NOT Friel's percentage formula). Backed by 2025 expert consensus (IJSPP): Z2 sits just below LT1; LT2 is the start of Z4.

**LT1 = 162 bpm** — derived from HR-pace deflection across a full year of training data (field estimate, not lab-verified)

**LT2 = 174 bpm** — derived from VDOT 45.7 (Jan 4 2026 10K at 4:28/km at altitude). Sits at 91% of HRmax — high end of typical 85-92% range but physiologically plausible.

**HRmax = 191 bpm** — highest confirmed reading across 262 activities (Sep 22 2025, threshold session). Garmin watch shows 195 but this is NOT confirmed by activity data — likely a historical spike pre-April 2025 or sensor artifact.

## Key Rules

- LTHR (bpm) stays roughly stable year to year; what improves with training is **pace at each zone**
- Zones don't need to change; re-test **threshold pace** periodically (20-30 min time trial) to track fitness
- Z4/Z5 boundary (182 bpm) is estimated at 95% of observed HRmax — confirm with 3-5 min all-out hill repeat + chest strap when healthy

## Training Distribution for Ultra Running (pyramidal, research-backed)

- Z1-Z2: ~75-80% of weekly volume
- Z3: ~15-20%
- Z4-Z5: ~5-8% (half-marathon effort or harder)
